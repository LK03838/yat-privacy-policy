DayRoot – Delete Your Account & Data

You can request deletion of your DayRoot account and all associated data by following these steps:

Option 1 — From the App

  1. Open DayRoot
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Sign Out
  4. Uninstall the app — all local data will be deleted

Option 2 — By Email

Send a deletion request to: lk03838@gmail.com

We will delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile (name, age, birthday)
  • All events and reminders
  • Family member data (spouse, children)
  • Google account link

Local device data is deleted immediately upon uninstall. Cloud data is deleted within 7 days of request.
