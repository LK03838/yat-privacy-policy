Privacy Policy for DayRoot

Last updated: May 16, 2026

DayRoot is a personal family and work organizer app. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data We Collect

  • Your name, age, and birthday (entered during onboarding)
  • Family member details (spouse, children) you add manually
  • Events and reminders you create
  • Google account email (only if you use Google Sign-In for backup)

How We Use Your Data

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • If you enable Google Sync, data is backed up to your personal Firebase account
  • We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising

Third Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — for optional cloud backup
  • Google Sign-In — for authentication only

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the app or signing out from Settings.
