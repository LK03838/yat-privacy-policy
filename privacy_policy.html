Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Venu Creations ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the YAT Location Alarm mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

The App requests access to your device's location (GPS) solely to monitor your current position and alert you when you arrive at your selected destination.

  • Location data — used only on your device in real time to calculate distance to your destination. It is never stored, transmitted, or shared with any server or third party.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

2. How We Use Location

Location access is used exclusively to:

  • Show your current position on the map
  • Calculate the distance between you and your chosen destination
  • Trigger an alarm notification when you arrive within your selected radius

Location is only accessed while the App is open and an alarm is active. We do not access your location in the background beyond this purpose.

3. Data Storage

All processing happens entirely on your device. No location data, destination information, or usage data is ever uploaded to any server or stored beyond your current session.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses OpenStreetMap tiles to display the map and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap's geocoding service) to search for place names. When you search for a destination, your search text is sent to Nominatim's servers to return matching locations. No personal or location data is included in these requests. Please refer to the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy for details.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
